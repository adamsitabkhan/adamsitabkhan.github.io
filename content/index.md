Hi! I'm Adam. 

Lately I've been thinking a lot about the concept of maintaining a "second brain". I've been in dire need of something to help me declutter the heaps of my quick notes, thoughts, and half-baked ideas that fill up my notes app. 

A course I'm taking this semester uses this Quartz 4 website builder, which I enjoy the visual simplicity of, so I'm stealing it for this site. What I appreciated more, though, was the philosophy behind it; the creator of Quartz wrote about maintaining a "digital garden", populated with musings of all shapes and sizes. 

I tend to be someone that overthinks the processes I use to achieve things that should be simple. It's a miracle that I've even gotten a few full sentences down instead of spending another 20 minutes figuring out which Markdown editor to use. At the end of the day, trying to fully optimize how you do something isn't worth it unless you actually sit down and *do* the thing, so I guess I'm off to a good start. 

So beyond declutter, what do I really want to get out of this? 

Well, I have a lot of ideas that I struggle to get off the ground, for reasons aforementioned. Additionally, I'm not satisfied with the ratio of the amount of content I consume vs. the amount of content I create. Writing is definitely a big part of that, particularly scientific communication. In exploring various topics I find interesting, I want to improve that ability, rather than just reading/watching, and afterwards forgetting. 

There's just way too much intellectual real estate out there to try to absorb it all, but putting the effort into maintaining this "second brain" would be a successful endeavor if it helped me make a few strides in areas where I hadn't explored before. 

At minimum, it would be cool if I used this space to compile resources that I personally use to explore topics in math, computer science, statistics, whatever tickles my fancy. A personal wiki of sorts. The next level would be to distill them in a way that I can use for my own personal reference in the future. The pinnacle of this site would be to synthesize information in a way where my contributions become greater than their original influences. This could look like an explanation of a topic that offers a new perspective, a visual that unlocks some new insight, or simply inspiring curiosity.

This has all been very stream-of-consciousness, but as I continue writing I hope I can structure this all more effectively. For now, I'll continue to build up this site and make it something I can be proud of :).
